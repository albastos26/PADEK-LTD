How it works

  1. Quote

    Tell us the site and what you need. You get a clear price before any drilling starts.

  2. Mobilise

    The rig drives onto ground trucks can’t reach. We aim to be on site within 48 hours of an agreed quote.

  3. Drill

    We drill to the agreed depth, up to 120 m, through soil and rock.

  4. Finish

    For water wells: casing, screening, pump fitting and a water quality test. Then handover.

Questions we get a lot

How deep can you drill?

The JK 650 reaches 120 m. What’s realistic at your site depends on the ground, so send us the location and we’ll tell you.

Do I get a price before you start?

Yes. You get a clear quote first, so you know what you’re paying for before the rig arrives.

Can you work on muddy, rocky or remote sites?

That’s what the crawler tracks are for. Remote and urban sites are both fine.
